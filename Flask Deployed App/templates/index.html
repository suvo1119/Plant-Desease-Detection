Why Detect Disease Early?

Plant diseases cause significant yield reduction if undetected. Proper identification of the disease-causing agent ensures targeted treatment, saving time, money, and preventing spread across your entire garden or field.

7 Essential Prevention Steps

  • Practice good garden sanitation & cleanup
  • Fertilize appropriately for strong immunity
  • Inspect new nursery plants carefully
  • Allow soil to warm before planting
  • Rotate annual crop families every season
  • Ensure good air circulation between plants
  • Promptly remove diseased stems & foliage
